## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Senior-Friendly Design**: High-contrast typography, readable fonts, warm glassmorphism UI, and 60 FPS hardware-accelerated Canvas graphics.
- **Zero Idle Load**: Optimized Canvas rendering loop that uses 0% CPU/GPU when idle, preserving mobile battery life.
- **100% Client-Side Engine**: Runs offline inside the browser with programmatic Web Audio sound effects and zero external asset downloads.
